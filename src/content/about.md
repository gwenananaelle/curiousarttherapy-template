Hello, Hola, Bonjour!
My name is Marie, and I am an art therapist, researcher, and artist with international experience supporting children, teens, adults, and families through illness, grief, neurodivergence, and life’s challenges. Based in Houston, I am provisionally licensed as a Professional Counselor Associate (LPC-A) and Art Therapist Registered Provisional (ATR-P).<br>

I hold a PhD in Expressive Therapies from Lesley University and a Master’s in Counseling and Art Therapy from Edgewood College. My clinical training includes work in pediatric and adult oncology at Hospital General de México and MD Anderson Cancer Center. I also developed Icanfeel, a digital tool that transforms patient-created art into health data, now in clinical trial at MD Anderson.<br>

In addition to clinical work, I serve as a visiting scholar at The New School University in New York, where I research the role of artificial intelligence in therapeutic attunement. I also teach at the Instituto Mexicano de Psicoterapia de Arte and serve on the leadership team of Expressive Arts Without Borders.<br>

As a practicing artist, I have exhibited in Paris, London, Mexico City, and Houston. I love weaving creativity into daily life, whether through painting, writing, or exploring new foods and cultures with my children.<br>

I bring a person-centered, multicultural perspective to everything I do, combining evidence-based methods such as cognitive behavioral therapy with creative, playful approaches to foster resilience, connection, and healing.
